# Flashtool Community Edition: Licensing and Attribution

## Original project

Flashtool was originally developed by Androxyde and contributors.

The upstream 0.9.34.0 source revision (`d4cba66`) contained GNU GPL version 3 licensing documentation and identified the application as GPLv3-or-later.

The original GPL text is preserved in `COPYING`.

## Community modifications

Flashtool Community Edition is an unofficial continuation of the original application.

Our modifications include native Apple Silicon support, macOS compatibility fixes, packaging improvements and dependency management.

Contributions authored by the community maintainers are intended to be distributed under GPLv3-or-later, to the extent that the contributors hold the necessary rights.

## Later upstream versions

The upstream 0.9.36 source no longer contains the original top-level licence declaration.

Existing valid GPL grants remain relevant to previously licensed code. However, this does not independently establish the licensing status of every addition made in later upstream revisions.

This question will be addressed before public binary distribution.

## Third-party components

Flashtool incorporates third-party libraries, native utilities and device-related resources.

These components may have different licences and redistribution conditions. The GPL licence of the principal application should not be interpreted as automatically granting redistribution rights over every bundled component.

Public release packages will include appropriate licensing notices and will exclude components for which the necessary rights cannot be established.

## Source availability

Public binary releases will be accompanied by the corresponding source code, modifications and build information required by the applicable licences.

## Independence

Flashtool Community Edition is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Androxyde or Sony.
